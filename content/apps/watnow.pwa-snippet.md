## Get the app on your iPhone (Safari)

This is a web app. On iPhone, you “install” it by saving it to your **Home Screen**.

> **Use Safari.** The “Add to Home Screen” option won’t show up the same way in other iPhone browsers.

<div class="pwa-install">

<ol class="pwa-steps">
  <li>
    <p><strong>Open Safari</strong></p>

      <img src="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-01-open-safari.png" alt="Step 1: Tap Safari." style="max-width: 360px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0.75rem auto;" />
    </a>
  </li>

  <li>
    <p><strong>Type the URL</strong>, then tap <strong>Go</strong></p>
    <a href="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-02-enter-url-and-go.png">
      <img src="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-02-enter-url-and-go.png" alt="Step 2: Enter watnow.netlify.app and tap Go." style="max-width: 360px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0.75rem auto;" />
    </a>
    <p><code>watnow.netlify.app</code></p>
    <p><a href="https://watnow.netlify.app/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open watnow.netlify.app</a></p>
  </li>

  <li>
    <p>Tap the <strong>Share</strong> button (square with arrow)</p>
    <a href="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-03-tap-share.png">
      <img src="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-03-tap-share.png" alt="Step 3: Tap the Share icon in Safari." style="max-width: 360px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0.75rem auto;" />
    </a>
  </li>

  <li>
    <p>Scroll a little and tap <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong></p>
    <a href="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-04-add-to-home-screen.png">
      <img src="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-04-add-to-home-screen.png" alt="Step 4: Tap Add to Home Screen in the share sheet." style="max-width: 360px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0.75rem auto;" />
    </a>
  </li>

  <li>
    <p>Tap <strong>Add</strong> (top-right). Optional: rename it first.</p>
    <a href="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-05-confirm-add.png">
      <img src="/images/apps/watnow/ios/install/step-05-confirm-add.png" alt="Step 5: Confirm the name and tap Add." style="max-width: 360px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0.75rem auto;" />
    </a>
  </li>
</ol>

<p style="margin-top: 1rem;"><strong>Done.</strong> You’ll now have an icon on your Home Screen that opens the app full‑screen.</p>
