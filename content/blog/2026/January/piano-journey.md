### I always wanted to play the piano

Getting to learn Piano is something I always want it to do.
Since I can remember music has been in my life. I played guitar and sang in the choir of my catolic school in Cuenca, La Sagrada Familia in Cuenca, Spain.mi mum went to the same school, so did I. I am an only child , loads of rules at home and not many joys but that is a story for a different day.I envy the fact that some people can sit at a piano and just play like if there was a language they understood and I did not know it existed.

I took classes of music reading twice, absolutely hated it as I did English and look at me now. I write in English with more fluency than I do in Spanish. (Maybe that is not such a high bar) point is I have now after years (2019 I started) good long years and cancer diagnosis and treatment in between plus I do not know how many house moves an d location changes within in England and Spain… I do play the piano almost daily.

I can remember when I sat on velvet turquoise stool that did not match the paint literally the first few weeks after I found this gem of instrument for free… Missing the point here sorry. I can remember how it felt the thought of being able to use both hands at the same time. That will never happen it is so difficult but how? Same as I used to think at age 11 of English and why do you need auxuliy thing sys like “do” what is this for? And again here I am typing and doing my life in English. Another Journey that I started way later than most people but did not mean that I would not learn it. I was 27 when I decided (and here is the key) I wanted to know how to learn English and committed to it.
