There are some conversations I cannot rush.

Yes, I could answer on the spot, but I do not want to. Not because I do not want to answer. Trust me, I do. Sometimes I have already seen the message and know how important it is. I am simply saving it for later.

I do the same with chocolate truffles. I search for the most delicious ones yet, then wait until I can sit in the quiet and truly enjoy them.

We are often told to pause before replying when we are angry, so that we do not merely react. I think the same can be true when we are excited, happy or deeply moved. Sometimes I need a moment, better said, I choose to take one to savour the message, and respond from a grounded, more attentive place.

Some conversations require that space. Some people deserve your full attention, even when they cannot see the care you are putting into your reply.

And I believe we can feel the difference. We can tell when someone answered in the rush of the moment, when they did not fully take in what we wrote, but mainly reacted to it.

Take your time. The person on the other side deserves it.
