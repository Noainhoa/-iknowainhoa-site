It is true that you can be gratefoul for terrible things happening to you.
Not at the time, or when crying after a procedure or when you do not recognise yourself in the mirror, clearly not there. 
hindsight, perspective those all come eventually.

I was diagnosed in 2022, June 8th, **"Why do you think you are here"** the Dr. said. In my mind I had know for while, I had gone for testing in January asking if I could have ovarian cancer as I had 4 of the symtops that were on a poster from Royal Mail. 

As the months went by I found myslef sinking in my head, I could not see the end of it. I realise I was going to bed with the problem and waking up with it, there was no scape, my body was it. And it hit me "My body is against me!" I could not buy another one, or take it to the mechanic and leave it there until they find waht is wrong with it. I had to be with it. 

Not many things help me, not many people were of confort. I felt that the "it sucks" resonated, so if someone was to say that I would say yes and feel more understood. 
I am bubble, chatter box, I have no need for positive kindered spirits I am one of those. At that time I needed to protest and say, "I am not having fun", "this is hard". 

It was manageable? yes. I did it? yes. Was it easier than it ever felt? 100%. But in the moment walking my dog was extraordinary breathless literallyy, going up hill was a half marathon feeling.
