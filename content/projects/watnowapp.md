# WhatNow

A lightweight web app designed to capture the moment something hard happens, then guide you back later to extract meaning and direction.

## Get the app on your iPhone (Safari)

On iPhone, the best way to use WhatNow is to save it to your Home Screen so it opens full-screen like a native app.

> **Use Safari.** The “Add to Home Screen” option won’t show up the same way in other iPhone browsers.

1. **Open Safari**  
   [![Open Safari from the Home Screen](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-01-open-safari.png)](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-01-open-safari.png)

2. **Go to the app URL** — type `watnow.netlify.app` and tap **Go**.  
   [![Type the URL in Safari and tap Go](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-02-enter-url-and-go.png)](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-02-enter-url-and-go.png)

3. **Tap the Share button** (square with an arrow).  
   [![Tap the Share icon in Safari](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-03-tap-share.png)](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-03-tap-share.png)

4. **Choose “Add to Home Screen.”**  
   [![Safari Share sheet with Add to Home Screen](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-04-add-to-home-screen.png)](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-04-add-to-home-screen.png)

5. **Tap “Add.”** You can keep or edit the name first.  
   [![Confirm the name and tap Add](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-05-confirm-add.png)](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-05-confirm-add.png)

**Optional tip:** Press-and-hold the Safari icon for quick actions (e.g., **New Tab**).  
[![Safari quick actions menu](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-06-optional-safari-shortcut.png)](/images/projects/watnow-ios-install-v2-annotated/step-06-optional-safari-shortcut.png)

## Quick actions (iPhone-ready)

1) Open: `https://watnow.netlify.app/`  
2) In Safari tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.  
3) Confirm **Add**.

## Why use it

- Process setbacks with structured prompts.
- Return to the same entry later to log what changed.
- Works on desktop and mobile; saves best as a Home Screen app on iPhone.

## Need the icon?

If you see a generic icon when adding to Home Screen, make sure your site has an Apple touch icon (often called `apple-touch-icon.png`) and/or a PWA manifest icon set.

- The screenshots above use the provided icon.  
- To wire a dedicated file, place your icon at `public/images/apps/watnow.png` and reference it in your PWA manifest / `apple-touch-icon`.
