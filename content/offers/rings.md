## Story {#story}

I made this ring because it was hard to carry silence into everyday life.

After my fourth Vipassana course, I began talking with my experience differently. I noticed how quickly the mind moved from one thing to the next, and how easy it was to miss the quiet edge of each moment.

The ring came from that gap: not to become another talisman, but to act as a small, tangible invitation to pause, feel weight, and return to what is here.

> “It’s not about owning a symbol; it’s about meeting what’s already on the table.”

The ring is simple by design. It does not promise change. It only offers a place to drop a fingertip and remember to pay attention.
